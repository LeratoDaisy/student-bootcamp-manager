    Register a student

    Enter the student's details. Their ID is created for you.

    Separate marks with commas or spaces. Each mark must be from 0 to 100. You can enter as many as you like.

    Good to know

    New students show up on the Students page with their average and PASS or FAIL already worked out.

    Use the Process button there to run a student through processing. It takes a moment, then the student is marked as done.

    If you leave the course blank, the student gets the default course. A student with no marks can be registered but can't be processed until they have marks.
